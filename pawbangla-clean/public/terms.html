Terms and safety limits · 17 October 2026

Use PawBangla with care

PawBangla is a community platform in development for animal welfare in Bangladesh. By signing in or using its forms, you agree to follow these rules.

Free adoption only. Buying, selling, prices, deposits, breeder ads and payment requests are not allowed. Submit only a genuine, permitted rehoming post.

Accurate, permitted submissions

  • Submit only truthful details and images you own or have permission to share.
  • Do not post another person’s private contact details, home address, threats, harassment, dangerous instructions, fake emergencies or content that may put a person or animal at risk.
  • Pet SOS reports publish immediately and are unverified. Reviewers are not available around the clock; a post or email alert does not mean anyone has seen it, that a clinic is open, or that rescue/help is on the way.
  • For urgent veterinary care, call a clinic directly. Do not rely on a website report or email alert as an emergency service.

Moderation and reports

Adoption posts and community photos require human approval. Reviewers may reject, hide or remove content that violates these rules or presents a safety, privacy or consent concern. Pet SOS posts publish immediately and may later be hidden or removed after a credible report. Use the site’s report control to flag abuse, incorrect contact details, a closed provider or a duplicate listing.

Identity checks

To protect adoption contact information, the pilot uses a private in-person document check. Reviewers record a status and note; they should not copy or store NID numbers or document images. This is not a government identity certification, background check or guarantee of anyone’s conduct. Contact details are shared only as shown on the site.

Directory information

Clinic and shop entries come from provider sources, maps or other directories and can become outdated. A listing, phone number, map link or description does not guarantee that a business exists, is open, offers a service, or will respond. Call before travelling.

Limits and responsibility

PawBangla connects users and shares community-provided information. It does not operate clinics, verify every report, arrange adoptions, dispatch rescuers or guarantee outcomes. Users are responsible for deciding what to share, checking information and meeting safely. Nothing on the site replaces professional veterinary advice or urgent local assistance.

Changes and contact

We may remove features or revise these terms as the pilot develops. Use Privacy to learn about data handling, Account controls to request deletion, or contact the owner through the email link in the footer.
