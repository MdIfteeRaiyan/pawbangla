ABOUT PAWBANGLA

Built with care for every animal.

I’m Iftee Raiyan, the creator of PawBangla. I started this project to make responsible rehoming, practical pet care and local help easier to find across Bangladesh.

One place for local help

Find free rehoming posts, veterinary clinics and pet shops, everyday care information, community photos and urgent lost, found or injured pet alerts.

Built to grow carefully

PawBangla is being expanded division by division. Directory entries show their source and what has actually been checked, so people can judge how much to rely on each detail.

Clear about our limits

We connect people and share information. We cannot guarantee a provider is open, an SOS receives help, a user’s claim is accurate or an adoption has a particular outcome.

PawBangla is a community-first project for Bangladesh. Adoption is always free rehoming—never buying or selling.

PRIVATE ID CHECK

Verify identity before sharing adoption contact.

Caretakers must be checked before posting. Prospective adopters must be checked before a caretaker’s number is shown. Show an original NID or other official photo ID to the designated reviewer in person. PawBangla stores the account name, email and check status only—not the NID number or a document image.

Request a private identity check

Sign in to PawBangla to request a private check. A reviewer will arrange a private in-person document check.

A check confirms only that a reviewer saw an original document and matched it to this account. It is not a government database or character check. Never email or post an ID image.

Pet SOS · please read

Help safely, with clear expectations.

Pet SOS is for lost, found and injured pets. Reports publish immediately without review. Read these safety notes before viewing or posting.

  • Share only useful location details.Use an area and nearby landmark. Never post a private home address; map pins are rounded before public display.
  • Protect people and animals.Share photos only with permission. Do not confront anyone or enter private property; contact local authorities or a vet when needed.
  • Reports are unverified—not a rescue guarantee.Anyone can view and report an alert. Sign-in, a 5-per-day limit and a truth confirmation reduce abuse, but cannot prevent it. PawBangla does not promise a response or rescue.
  • For urgent care, call a vet directly.PawBangla is a community notice service, not a rescue or emergency-dispatch service. Email alerts require opt-in and can be turned off any time.
