Privacy notice · 17 October 2026

Your data and choices

PawBangla is a community service for free pet adoption, care discovery, pet photos and lost, found or injured animal reports. This notice describes the information the current site collects and how to ask us to remove it.

Sharing photos is your choice. Only share images and details you have permission to share. Approved community photos and active Pet SOS reports can be visible to anyone. PawBangla cannot control screenshots, copying, or how other people use public information.

Information we receive

  • Sign-in: a ChatGPT sign-in provides an account identifier and email address so the site can protect submissions and reviewer access.
  • Adoption: caretaker name, pet details, area, care notes, WhatsApp number, image and review status. Pending listings and their contact details are private to reviewers. After approval, the phone is shown only to accounts that passed PawBangla’s in-person identity check.
  • Community: pet name, caption and photo. New photos stay private while pending; approved photos and captions appear publicly.
  • Pet SOS: animal description, event time, area, optional landmark and photo, reporter phone, and map coordinates. Reports publish immediately without pre-review. The phone is public; public coordinates are rounded to an approximate area.
  • Email alerts: if you opt in, the site stores your account ID, email, subscription time and unsubscribe token. Alert emails include the report details and a link to unsubscribe. The site stores reviewer queue records and delivery status to support retries.
  • Reviews and identity checks: private abuse or directory reports and their resolution notes; for adoption access, your name, email, check status, request time and reviewer note. PawBangla does not ask reviewers to store NID numbers or document images.

How we use it

We use these details to display approved posts, enforce the no-buying/no-selling rule, restrict adoption contact access, send opt-in SOS alerts, prevent abuse, correct directory entries and review safety reports. We do not use pending images in the public gallery.

Who can see it

Designated reviewers can see private submissions, contact details needed for review, identity-check requests, reports and delivery failures. Public visitors can see approved adoption details, approved community photos and live SOS reports as described above. Email providers process messages when sending is configured. PawBangla’s hosting and storage providers process site data to operate the service.

How long it is kept

The current site has no automatic retention schedule. Submissions and reviewer records remain until they are removed through the account deletion tool or handled by a reviewer for safety or service reasons. Email delivery records are retained to support delivery and retries; unsubscribing removes the subscription and unsent queue entries. Hosting providers may retain routine backups under their own service terms. We do not claim immediate removal from copies made by other people or from provider backups.

Your choices and requests

  • Do not submit a photo or report if you are not comfortable with the stated visibility.
  • Turn off alert emails from the Pet SOS section or use the unsubscribe link in a message.
  • Use Delete my PawBangla data while signed in to remove your submissions, images, reports, alert subscription and identity-check record.
  • Use the report controls on a listing or email the creator through the footer to report a privacy or safety issue.

Deleting PawBangla data does not delete your ChatGPT account, emails already sent, or information another person copied. For an unresolved request, contact the PawBangla owner by email.

Updates

We will revise this page when data handling changes. The date above indicates the current revision. This notice explains the current prototype; it is not a legal compliance certification.
