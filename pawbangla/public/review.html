PawBangla · Content review

Private queue · Adoption listings and community photos need human approval. Pet SOS reports publish immediately without pre-review.

Private review rules: Unapproved photos and unapproved contact details are visible only to designated reviewers. Public APIs and images must expose approved items only; approved contact details appear only after explicit consent. Never copy a submitter’s phone, email or home address into a public caption.

Adoption · free rehoming only

  • Approve only when caretaker authority/permission and public WhatsApp consent are present, the animal details are useful, and the image is safe.
  • Reject buying, selling, “rehoming fees,” prices, deposits, payment requests, breeding/stud ads, trades, or attempts to move a sale off-site.
  • Reject or ask for correction if ownership is unclear, the photo is stolen/unsafe, information exposes a private address, or the post is not an actual free rehoming.

Community photos

  • Approve only a permitted pet photo with a safe caption and no sales, personal phone/email, home address, harassment, or identifying details that put someone at risk.
  • Reject copied images, harmful/graphic content, private images shared without consent, ads, and captions that expose a person or location.
  • Hide an already-published photo while investigating a credible privacy, consent, or safety complaint.

Pet SOS · lost/found/injured animals

  • SOS posts publish immediately; there is no pre-review queue. The submitter confirms accuracy and consents to public phone, area and optional photo.
  • Never promise a rescue response. Public map pins are rounded. Fake, duplicate, sales, doxxing, threats or dangerous misinformation reports may be hidden.
  • Review community flags promptly when available; urgent danger should be reported to local emergency services or veterinary care. The site does not provide 24/7 dispatch.
Decision path: approve only after every checklist item is checked; reject/remove a submission that clearly breaks these rules; hide a public item while a credible safety or privacy concern is investigated; escalate uncertain, repeat, threatening, or potentially criminal reports. Users can use “Report” on posts or “Report wrong details / closed / duplicate” on directory cards. Reports are private and require sign-in. Only the reported business phone should be included when correcting a listing; never add your own phone, email or home address. The on-duty reviewer handles first triage. The PawBangla site owner is the lead reviewer for this pilot and owns unresolved or escalated reports until a resolution is recorded. Do not promise rescue, clinic availability, or a response time; direct urgent animal-care needs to a clinic or appropriate local authority.
