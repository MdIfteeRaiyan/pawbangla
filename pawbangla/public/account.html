Delete your PawBangla data

This permanently removes your PawBangla adoption posts and photos, community posts and photos, Pet SOS reports and photos, private reports you filed, email alert subscription, and identity-check record associated with the signed-in account.

It does not delete your Firebase Authentication account, messages already delivered by email, copied or screenshotted public information, or routine hosting-provider backups. Deletion is available only after signing in with the same PawBangla account used to submit the data.

Read the privacy notice before continuing. You can also contact the site owner via the email link on the home page.
